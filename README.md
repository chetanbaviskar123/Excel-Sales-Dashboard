# 📊Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Excel

## 📌Project Overview: 
This project is an interactive Sales Dashboard built entirely in Microsoft Excel. It transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Excel Dashboard techniques. The dashboard enables users to analyze sales performance across different years, categories, states, customers, and months for better decision-making.
The dataset contains transactional sales records with information such as Order Date, Customer Name, State, Category, Sub-Category, Product Name, Sales, Quantity, and Profit.

## 🎯Objectives:
Analyze overall sales performance.
Track profit trends over time.
Compare sales across product categories.
Identify top-performing customers.
Visualize sales by state.
Monitor monthly sales trends.
Provide interactive filtering using slicers.

## ❓ Business Questions (KPIs):

This dashboard answers the following business questions:

Which product sub-category generates the highest sales?
How has profit changed over the years?
Which states contribute the highest sales?
How many customers were acquired each year?
Who are the top 5 profit-generating customers?
What is the monthly sales trend?
How do sales vary across different product categories?
Which year recorded the best overall business performance?

## ⚙️ Process:
Data Collection – Imported the sales dataset containing orders, customers, products, sales, quantity, and profit.
Data Cleaning – Checked data quality, formatted columns, and prepared the dataset for analysis.
Data Analysis – Created Pivot Tables to summarize sales, profit, customers, states, and monthly trends.
Dashboard Creation – Designed interactive charts, maps, and KPIs using Pivot Charts and Slicers.
Insights Generation – Analyzed business performance to identify trends, top-performing products, customers, and regions.

## 📈 Sales Dashboard Insights:
1. Storage and Binders are the Highest Revenue Generators
The Storage category generated the highest sales (≈ 50.3K), followed by Binders (≈ 43.5K).
These two sub-categories contribute significantly to overall revenue.
Business Insight: Focus on inventory availability and marketing campaigns for these products to maximize sales.
2. Profit Shows Consistent Growth Over Time
Profit increased steadily from 2014 to 2017.
The Technology category experienced the strongest profit growth among all categories.
Business Insight: Technology products are becoming increasingly profitable and deserve greater investment.
3. Sales are Concentrated in Specific States
The state-wise map indicates that a few states contribute much higher sales than others.
Several states show relatively low sales performance.
Business Insight: Expand marketing efforts in underperforming regions while strengthening operations in high-performing states.
4. Customer Base Increased Every Year
Customer count grew consistently from 2014 to 2017.
2017 recorded the highest number of customers.
Business Insight: Customer acquisition strategies are working effectively and should continue to be optimized.
5. Revenue is Concentrated Among Top Customers
The Top 5 Customers account for a significant share of total profits.
The leading customer alone contributes over half of the profit among the top five.
Business Insight: Develop customer retention programs and personalized offers for high-value customers.
6. Monthly Sales Show Seasonal Trends
Sales are relatively low during the first quarter.
Strong peaks occur around September and November, followed by a decline in October.
Business Insight: Prepare inventory and promotional campaigns before high-demand months to maximize revenue.
7. Office Supplies Dominate Sales Volume
Office Supply sub-categories such as Storage, Binders, Paper, and Appliances contribute heavily to total sales.
Business Insight: Maintaining stock levels and competitive pricing in Office Supplies can significantly improve revenue.
8. Dashboard Enables Interactive Analysis
Users can filter data by Category and Year using slicers.
This allows quick comparison of sales performance across different business segments and years.

## 📈 Business Recommendations:
Increase inventory for high-demand products such as Storage and Binders.
Invest more in the Technology category due to its strong profitability.
Expand marketing efforts in underperforming states.
Strengthen loyalty programs for top customers.
Plan promotional campaigns before peak sales months.
Monitor yearly sales trends to support strategic planning.
